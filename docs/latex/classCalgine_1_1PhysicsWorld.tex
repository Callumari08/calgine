\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
The engine\textquotesingle{}s physics simulation (backed by Jolt Physics). 

The \doxylink{classCalgine_1_1App}{App} owns the lifecycle: the world is created in \doxylink{classCalgine_1_1App_a52ea702157554748e98b84dc0132e39f}{App::\+systems\+\_\+init()}, stepped once per fixed timestep right after every \doxylink{classCalgine_1_1Behaviour}{Behaviour}\textquotesingle{}s fixed\+\_\+update\+\_\+tick(), and shut down when the \doxylink{classCalgine_1_1App}{App} is destroyed.

Add a \doxylink{classCalgine_1_1RigidBody}{Rigid\+Body} behaviour to a \doxylink{classCalgine_1_1GameObject}{Game\+Object} to give it a body in this world. Collisions are reported through the \doxylink{classCalgine_1_1EventContext}{Event\+Context} as \doxylink{structCalgine_1_1CollisionEvent}{Collision\+Event} (see \doxylink{collision__event_8h}{collision\+\_\+event.\+h}).
