\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Submitted to the \doxylink{classCalgine_1_1EventContext}{Event\+Context} by the physics system when two Rigid\+Bodies start or stop touching. 

Events are submitted during the fixed\+\_\+update phase (right after the physics step) and stay readable for the rest of that frame: later fixed steps, update, late\+\_\+update, render, imgui\+\_\+render and final. They are cleared when the next frame enters fixed\+\_\+update.
