\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Settings used when a \doxylink{classCalgine_1_1Joint}{Joint} connects its bodies. 

Anchors and axes are local to their \doxylink{classCalgine_1_1GameObject}{Game\+Object} by default, and are converted to world space once, when the joint is created.
